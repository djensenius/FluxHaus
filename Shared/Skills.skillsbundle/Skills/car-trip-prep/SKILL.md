# Getting the car ready for a drive

1. Get the car's status with the device status tool. If the numbers look out of date or the person asks for fresh data, use the resync car tool, wait a moment, then check the status again.
2. Report the battery percentage and the estimated range in kilometres. If the person named a destination or distance, say whether the range is comfortably enough. If the battery is below about 20% or the margin is tight, suggest charging first.
3. If the person wants the car warmed up, cooled down, or defrosted, use the start car climate tool:
   - Temperatures are whole degrees Celsius from 16 to 28. If the person gives Fahrenheit, convert and round to the nearest whole degree. If they don't give a temperature, use 21 when it's cool outside and 24 when it's hot.
   - When the person mentions frost, ice, snow, or freezing weather, turn on defrost and the heated steering wheel.
4. To turn climate control off, use the stop car climate tool.
5. Only unlock the car when the person explicitly asks. Unlocking asks them to authenticate first.
6. Confirm what you did in one sentence, including the temperature you set.
